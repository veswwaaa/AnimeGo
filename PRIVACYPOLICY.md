# Privacy Policy — AnimeGo

Effective date: [18/11/2026]

AnimeGo ("the App") does not sell or share personal data with third parties.

## What we collect
- Your MyAnimeList account identifier (username) and an encrypted session token, stored only on your device in the platform keystore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore).
- Your anime list entries, watch status, scores and episode progress.

We do not store your MyAnimeList password. Authentication is handled by MyAnimeList's official OAuth flow.

## Third parties
- MyAnimeList (API access, subject to the MyAnimeList API Terms of Use)
- Anime metadata and streaming links are retrieved from public third-party providers.

## Your rights
You can review and delete your data at any time by clearing app data or signing out. To request deletion, contact [email].

## Children
The App is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes
We may update this policy; material changes will be announced in the App.
